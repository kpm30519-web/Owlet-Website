Review & Edit Your Recipes

Recipe webpages aren't always perfectly formatted. Owlet gives you a chance to clean up the captured recipe before you save it.

Review the captured recipe and make changes before saving.

Capturing a recipe is only the beginning. Websites can contain extra text, unusual formatting, missing information, or details you simply don't want in your personal recipe collection.

Owlet puts the captured recipe into an editable review so you can make corrections before committing it to your recipe manager.

Clean up the details

Depending on the recipe, you can review ingredients, instructions, servings, tags, notes, and other recipe fields. Fix a typo, adjust a serving count, remove unwanted text, or make the recipe fit the way you actually cook.

Review before you save

The review step also gives you a chance to catch anything the webpage did not provide cleanly. If something needs attention, you can correct it before sending the recipe to KitchenOwl or exporting it locally.
